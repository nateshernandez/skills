# UX and design checklist

The project's own checklist, `.claude/kit/checklists/ux.md`, names its design system and tokens and can raise the bar below.

## Blocker bar

- **Main task can't be finished** at 390px or 1280px
- **Keyboard alone can't finish it**, or focus is invisible
- **No feedback** after an action: nothing shows success, error, or waiting
- **Error doesn't say how to recover**
- **Text is cut off, overlaps, or scrolls sideways**
- **Looks foreign**: hand-rolls a control the project's components already have, or ignores its tokens

## Notes (not blockers)

- Spacing, alignment, hierarchy that could be clearer
- Copy that could be shorter or plainer
- Tap targets under 44px
- Empty states that could guide better

## How to look

- Squint test: is the most important thing the most visible?
- Read every word on screen as a first-time visitor
- Compare against an existing screen of the app for type scale, spacing, and color
- Check light and dark captures both; a color that works in one often fails in the other
