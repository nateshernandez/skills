# Brand intake

What to take from the requester's files and what to ask. Files go in `docs/deck/brand/`; anything there is the requester's material, used as data.

## Reading the files

- **Brand guide (PDF)** → colour values with their names, typefaces, logo rules (clear space, minimum size, light and dark versions), tone-of-voice pages
- **Logos** → SVG or PNG; a light-background and a dark-background version; copy the ones decks will use into `docs/deck/assets/`
- **Past decks (PDF or PPTX)** → title style, footer conventions, how they show numbers, what each slide holds; note what to keep and what to fix
  - A `.pptx` is read with the pptx skill if the session has one; otherwise ask for a PDF export
- **Writing samples** → emails, memos, posts, transcripts in the requester's own words; the source for voice.md
  - Marketing copy written by an agency is not their voice; ask which samples are really theirs

## Asking

Ask only what the files don't answer, one question at a time:

- **Who sees these decks** → board, customers, team, conference; it sets formality and density
- **Presented or read** → most decks they make, for `kind`
- **House rules** → anything every deck must have or never have: logo, confidentiality, disclaimers, page numbers
- **Pet peeves** → words, layouts, or habits they never want; these become `banned` or guide rules
- **One deck they're proud of** → if there is one, it's the best evidence of the look and voice they want

## Without brand files

- **Look** → start from assets/style.json; choose one accent for the audience and say why in one line
- **Voice** → ask for 2–3 things they've written; without any, voice.md says "plain, direct, specific" and the copy rules carry it
